Logic Levels

Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what the Logic Levels app ("the app") stores about you and why. The app is a hobby project. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

What we store

  • An account ID. When you open the app, an anonymous account is created so your results can appear on the leaderboards. It isn't linked to your identity unless you sign in with Google.
  • Your leaderboard name. The name you choose is shown publicly on the leaderboards.
  • Game results. For each ranked level you clear: your time, number of moves, the number of attempts and when you cleared it. Also how many practice puzzles you have solved in each game. These are used for the leaderboards and your statistics. Times, moves and practice counts are shown publicly next to your leaderboard name.
  • Google account details (only if you sign in with Google). Google gives us your email address, your name and a Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in, keep your progress across phones, and suggest your first name as a leaderboard name. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Your progress is also saved on your phone, so the app works offline.

Where it is stored

Account and game data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the app's database and sign-in. Google sign-in is provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and all of its data at any time (see below). Uninstalling the app removes the data saved on your phone.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing juzt.gamez.inc@gmail.com. Please include your leaderboard name, or the Google email you signed in with. We will reply within 30 days. You can change your leaderboard name at any time in the app's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has signed in with Google, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
