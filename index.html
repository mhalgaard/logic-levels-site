Logic Levels

Endless logic puzzles. Same levels for everyone.

Logic Levels is a free puzzle game for Android. Climb an endless ladder of levels. Every level is identical for every player, so you can compare your time and moves on the leaderboards. Only your first clear of a level counts.

▶ Play in your browser

Works on any phone or computer, iPhone included. Same levels and leaderboards as the Android app.

Games

  • TubesSort the coloured balls so every tube holds one colour.
  • KingsOne king per row, column and colour area, and no two kings touching.
  • PatternsReveal a hidden picture from the number clues.
  • PipesRotate pipes to bring water to every drain.
  • TrailDraw one line through every cell, passing the numbers in order.
  • ArrowsSlide arrows off the board into boxes of their colour.

Practice mode adds endless puzzles of every game at Easy, Medium, Hard and Insane, plus Sudoku and Words.

Accounts

You can play as a guest under a name you choose, or sign in with Google to keep your progress across phones. See the privacy policy for what is stored.
